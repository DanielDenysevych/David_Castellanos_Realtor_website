About me

I know what it is like to land somewhere new and start from scratch.

I am Colombian-Canadian, and Brandon has been home for more than a decade. I first came to Canada at thirteen, with my mother. I lived in Vancouver and then Toronto before moving here in 2009 to continue my position with the Canadian Armed Forces.

So I know first-hand how hard relocating is, and how much it matters to have honest, reliable information when you are trying to build a foundation in a community you do not know yet. What I want for my clients is simple: a sense of safety and comfort while they look for a home. I own several properties in Brandon myself, and I draw on that experience as an owner to give people the full picture they deserve. I offer services in English and Spanish.

Away from my work as a REALTOR®, I am committed to supporting youth in Brandon, and I spend the rest of my time with my family.
